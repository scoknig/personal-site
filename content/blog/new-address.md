***Quick housekeeping note*:** \
**[tl;dr] This site has moved from `scottk.mba` to `sknight.xyz`.**

The `.mba` domain carried, at least for me, a subtle implication that this was some kind of professional or business showcase, which was never really the intent. This has always been meant as a catch-all personal site—whatever I'm reading, building, or thinking about, not just career stuff. `sknight.xyz` is simply a more neutral home for that. As an added bonus, it's also cheaper to keep renewed.

If you've got the old domain bookmarked or you're subscribed via [RSS](https://sknight.xyz/blog/index.xml) (I'm flattered), you don't need to do anything for now. `scottk.mba` now redirects to `sknight.xyz` automatically and preserves the full path, so an old link like `scottk.mba/dont-wait-to-be-free` will still take you to the right post instead of confoundingly dumping you on the homepage.

*For anyone technical who cares about the details:*

I had to be careful with the RSS feed here. Feed readers track what they've already shown you by each post's `guid`, which is usually just the post's URL. If I'd let that regenerate naively under the new domain, every post I've ever published would've gotten a new guid overnight and probably surfaced as "unread" all at once for anyone subscribed. To avoid that, every post published before today keeps its original `scottk.mba` guid, frozen in place and marked as a non-permalink identifier rather than a real link. Anything I publish from here on gets a domain-independent guid instead, so this isn't a problem again if I ever move domains in the future.

Everything else is unchanged, same me, just a new address. Update your bookmarks whenever you get around to it, and thanks for reading.
